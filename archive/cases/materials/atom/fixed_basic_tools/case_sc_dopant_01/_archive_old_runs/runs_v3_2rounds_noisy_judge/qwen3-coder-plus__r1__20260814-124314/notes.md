## Pattern Analysis

1. Tc decreases monotonically with increasing Pr substitution in both RE families.
2. The Nd family shows a more rapid suppression of Tc compared to the Er family at the same Pr concentration.
3. The Er family maintains higher Tc values at low Pr concentrations compared to the Nd family.
4. Both families reach zero Tc at high Pr concentrations (around 0.5-0.9), indicating loss of superconductivity.

## Prediction Methodology for Gd Family

The Gd family likely exhibits Tc suppression behavior intermediate between Er and Nd families. Based on lanthanide trends and ionic radii considerations:
- Gd has an ionic radius between Er and Nd
- Therefore, the Tc suppression rate should be between that of Er and Nd
- Specifically, Gd should show faster Tc suppression than Er but slower than Nd

My predicted Tc values for Gd family follow this intermediate trend, positioned between the Er and Nd values for each Pr concentration.

## Reasoning

Gd is positioned between Er and Nd in the lanthanide series in terms of ionic radius. This suggests that the Tc suppression due to Pr doping will follow an intermediate trend between these two families. For each Pr concentration, I estimated the Tc value to be approximately halfway between the Er and Nd values, adjusted for the observed trend patterns.
