# Notes on Tc Prediction for Gd Family Cuprates

## Measurements Taken

I measured several compositions from both the Er and Nd families to understand how Tc varies with Pr content and how it differs between RE families:

### Er Family Measurements:
- O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.030Er0.970: Tc = 93.9 K
- O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.150Er0.850: Tc = 82.9 K  
- O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.200Er0.800: Tc = 76.4 K
- O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.300Er0.700: Tc = 61.1 K
- O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.500Er0.500: Tc = 28.6 K
- O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.900Er0.100: Tc = 0.0 K

### Nd Family Measurements:
- O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.030Nd0.970: Tc = 85.1 K
- O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.150Nd0.850: Tc = 40.7 K
- O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.200Nd0.800: Tc = 31.4 K
- O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.300Nd0.700: Tc = 5.6 K
- O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.500Nd0.500: Tc = 0.0 K
- O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.900Nd0.100: Tc = 0.0 K

## Pattern Analysis

From the measurements, I can see that:
1. Both RE families show a decrease in Tc with increasing Pr content
2. The Nd family shows a steeper decline in Tc with Pr doping compared to the Er family
3. At low Pr content (0.030), both families have high Tc (Er: 93.9K, Nd: 85.1K)
4. At higher Pr content (0.150), the difference becomes more pronounced (Er: 82.9K, Nd: 40.7K)
5. The Nd family reaches Tc = 0.0K at lower Pr content than the Er family

## Reasoning for Gd Family Predictions

Based on the periodic trends, Gadolinium (Gd) sits between Erbium (Er) and Neodymium (Nd) in the lanthanide series in terms of ionic radius. Since the Tc suppression by Pr appears to depend on the RE ion size (with smaller ions showing stronger suppression), I expect the Gd family to show intermediate behavior between Er and Nd.

Specifically, since Gd has an ionic radius between Er and Nd, I expect the Tc vs Pr curve for Gd to fall between the Er and Nd curves - closer to the Er curve but slightly steeper than Er and less steep than Nd. 

Looking at the data points, I can estimate where Gd should fall by interpolating between the Er and Nd values, leaning slightly towards the Nd behavior due to Gd's position in the lanthanide series.
