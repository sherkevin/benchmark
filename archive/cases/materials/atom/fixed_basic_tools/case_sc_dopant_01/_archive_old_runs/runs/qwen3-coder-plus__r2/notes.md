## Observed Pattern

The data shows that Tc decreases as Pr content increases for both Er and Nd series. However, the rate of decrease differs between the two rare earth elements:

- For Er series: Tc drops gradually with increasing Pr content, reaching 0 at Pr=0.800
- For Nd series: Tc drops more rapidly with increasing Pr content, reaching 0 at Pr=0.500

Based on the behavior of the Er and Nd series, I can infer that Gd will likely show similar behavior to Er and Nd, with Tc decreasing as Pr content increases. Since Gd is between Nd and Er in the lanthanide series, I expect its behavior to be somewhere between these two.

Looking at the trend, the critical Pr content (where Tc becomes 0) seems to be around 0.500 for Nd and 0.800 for Er. For Gd, I expect it to be somewhere in between, likely closer to 0.600-0.700.

For predicting Tc values of Gd series, I'll interpolate based on the observed trends in the Er and Nd series.
