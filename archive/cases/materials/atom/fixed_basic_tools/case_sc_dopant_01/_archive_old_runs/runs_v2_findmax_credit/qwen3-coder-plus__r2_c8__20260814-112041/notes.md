# Notes on Finding Highest-Tc Composition

## Initial Analysis
I have 30 candidate compositions with 8 measurement credits. The compositions follow a pattern:
- Base: O7Cu3Ba2
- Doped with varying ratios of Pr and one of three rare earth elements: Er, Gd, or Nd

The compositions seem to vary systematically with different Pr concentrations (from 0.030 to 0.900) and corresponding rare earth element concentrations (from 0.970 to 0.100).

## Strategy
I'll use an adaptive approach:
1. Start by measuring a few compositions to identify trends
2. Focus on compositions that seem promising based on initial measurements
3. Look for patterns in how Tc varies with Pr concentration and rare earth element type
