# Measurement Strategy

## Initial Analysis
Looking at the 30 candidate compositions, I can see they follow a pattern:
- Base: O7Cu3Ba2
- Fixed small amount of Pr (either 0.030, 0.076/0.078/0.080, or increments of 0.05 up to 0.900)
- Variable amounts of Er, Gd, or Nd to fill the rest

There seem to be 3 main categories based on rare earth dopant:
- Er-doped compounds
- Gd-doped compounds  
- Nd-doped compounds

And different Pr concentrations:
- Low: 0.030
- Medium: ~0.076-0.080
- Higher increments: 0.150, 0.200, 0.300, 0.400, 0.500, 0.600, 0.700, 0.800, 0.900

## Strategy
With 12 credits and 30 candidates, I need to strategically sample to identify trends.
I'll use a systematic approach:
1. Measure 3 samples from each dopant category at different Pr concentrations to identify trends
2. Focus remaining measurements on the most promising category
3. Then fine-tune to find the peak

## Analysis After 9 Measurements

Results:
- Er-doped compounds: 93.9K (0.030Pr), 28.6K (0.500Pr), 0.0K (0.900Pr)
- Gd-doped compounds: 93.1K (0.030Pr), 0.0K (0.500Pr), 0.0K (0.900Pr)
- Nd-doped compounds: 85.1K (0.030Pr), 0.0K (0.500Pr), 0.0K (0.900Pr)

Clear trend: Low Pr concentration gives highest Tc, especially for Er-doped compounds.
The Er-doped compound with 0.030Pr (and 0.970Er) gives the highest Tc so far at 93.9K.
