# Measurement Notes — Pr-doped REBCO Superconductor Search

## System
Pr-doped REBa₂Cu₃O₇ (REBCO) cuprate superconductors. Formula: O7Cu3Ba2Pr_x RE_(1-x), where RE ∈ {Er, Gd, Nd}. 30 candidates spanning Pr from 0.030 to 0.900.

## Measurement Strategy
With 16 credits and 30 candidates, I used a structured exploration:

### Round 1 — Broad survey (8 measurements)
Sampled across Pr levels (low/mid/high) and all three RE elements to identify trends.

| # | Composition | Pr | RE | Tc (K) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.030Er0.970 | 0.030 | Er | **93.9** |
| 2 | O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.030Gd0.970 | 0.030 | Gd | 93.1 |
| 3 | O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.030Nd0.970 | 0.030 | Nd | 85.1 |
| 4 | O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.150Er0.850 | 0.150 | Er | 82.9 |
| 5 | O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.150Gd0.850 | 0.150 | Gd | 67.9 |
| 6 | O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.150Nd0.850 | 0.150 | Nd | 40.7 |
| 7 | O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.500Er0.500 | 0.500 | Er | 28.6 |
| 8 | O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.900Er0.100 | 0.900 | Er | 0.0 |

**Round 1 findings:**
1. Tc drops sharply and monotonically with increasing Pr content (Pr suppresses superconductivity — consistent with known "Pr effect" in cuprates)
2. At every Pr level: Er gives highest Tc, Gd intermediate, Nd lowest
3. The Pr=0.030 Er candidate (93.9 K) is the early leader

### Round 2 — Focused follow-up (6 measurements)
Filled in the Er curve and checked low-Pr Gd/Nd candidates.

| # | Composition | Pr | RE | Tc (K) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.076Er0.924 | 0.076 | Er | 91.1 |
| 10 | O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.200Er0.800 | 0.200 | Er | 76.4 |
| 11 | O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.300Er0.700 | 0.300 | Er | 61.1 |
| 12 | O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.400Er0.600 | 0.400 | Er | 45.8 |
| 13 | O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.078Gd0.922 | 0.078 | Gd | 84.0 |
| 14 | O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.080Nd0.920 | 0.080 | Nd | 68.2 |

**Round 2 findings:**
- Er series is strictly monotonically decreasing: 93.9 → 91.1 → 82.9 → 76.4 → 61.1 → 45.8 → 28.6 → 0.0
- Gd at Pr=0.078 gives 84.0 K (below Er at Pr=0.030 = 93.9 K)
- Nd at Pr=0.080 gives 68.2 K (far below Er leader)
- All low-Pr candidates measured confirm the Er at Pr=0.030 is the global maximum

## Conclusion
14 of 16 credits used. 2 remaining credits are unnecessary — the answer is unambiguous.

**Winner: O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.030Er0.970 with Tc = 93.9 K**

Reasoning: Tc is maximized at the lowest Pr doping (0.030) with the most favorable rare earth (Er). Both trends (lower Pr → higher Tc, and Er > Gd > Nd) are monotonic and consistent across all measured points. No unmeasured candidate can plausibly exceed 93.9 K, since the only lower-Pr candidates don't exist in the list, and all other RE elements at Pr=0.030 give lower Tc.
