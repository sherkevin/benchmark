# High-Tc Composition Search - Experimental Log

## Initial Observations
- 30 candidate compositions: O7Cu3Ba2Pr_xR_(1-x) where R ∈ {Er, Gd, Nd}
- Pr doping levels range from x = 0.030 to 0.900
- Three rare earth series: Er (rows 1, 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 18,166, 25, 28)
- Gd (rows 2, 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 19, 21, 23, 26, 29)
- Nd (rows 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 20, 22, 24, 27, 30)

## Hypothesis
In cuprate superconductors, Pr doping often suppresses superconductivity (known as "Pr suppression" in high-Tc superconductors). The optimal doping level is likely somewhere in the low to moderate range (0.03-0.15). Different rare earth elements may have different optimal doping levels due to their ionic radii and magnetic properties.

## Final Analysis (after all 12 measurements)

### Trend Summary:
1. **Er-based compounds**: Highest baseline Tc (93.9 K at x=0.030), moderate suppression with increasing Pr doping:
   - x=0.030: 93.9 K
   - x=0.076: 91.1 K
   - x=0.150: 82.9 K
   - x=0.200: 76.4 K
   - x=0.300: 61.1 K
   Clear monotonic suppression trend.

2. **Gd-based compounds**: Good baseline Tc (93.1 K at x=0.030), stronger suppression than Er:
   - x=0.030: 93.1 K
   - x=0.078: 84.0 K
   - x=0.150: 67.9 K
   - x=0.200: 57.7 K

3. **Nd-based compounds**: Lowest baseline Tc (85.1 K at x=0.030), strongest suppression:
   - x=0.030: 85.1 K
   - x=0.080: 68.2 K
   - x=0.150: 40.7 K

### Key Findings:
- All systems show monotonic suppression of Tc with increasing Pr doping
- No evidence of optimal doping above x=0.030 in any system
- Er shows the highest Tc at all comparable doping levels
- The suppression rate follows: Nd > Gd > Er

### Final Best Candidate: O7Cu3Ba2Pr0.030Er0.970 (93.9 K)

### Reasoning:
The lowest Pr doping (x=0.030) gives the highest Tc across all rare earth systems. Among these, Er-based compounds show the highest Tc (93.9 K). Higher Pr doping monotonically suppresses Tc in all systems, with no evidence of enhancement at any doping level. Therefore, the composition with minimal Pr doping in the Er system is the optimal choice.

## Experimental Strategy
1. Start with baseline measurements to understand the trend
2. Focus on low doping levels first (where Tc is likely highest)
3. Compare across rare earth elements to see which system responds best to Pr doping
4. Use remaining credits to refine search around promising regions
