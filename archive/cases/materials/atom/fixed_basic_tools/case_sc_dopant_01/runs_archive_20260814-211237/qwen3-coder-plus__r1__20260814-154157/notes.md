## Pattern Analysis

1. **General Trend**: As Pr content increases, Tc decreases for both RE families.
2. **Initial Tc Values**: Pure Er-based compound starts at ~94K, pure Nd-based compound starts at ~85K.
3. **Rate of Suppression**: The Nd family shows more rapid suppression of Tc compared to the Er family.
   - At 20% Pr substitution: Er-family retains ~76K Tc, Nd-family drops to ~31K
   - At 30% Pr substitution: Er-family retains ~61K Tc, Nd-family drops to ~5.6K
   - Complete suppression occurs at lower Pr content in Nd family (around 50%) than in Er family (around 80%)

## Interpolation for Gd Family

Looking at the periodic table positioning and known properties:
- Gd (Gadolinium) sits between Nd and Er in the lanthanide series
- Based on the trends observed, Gd would likely exhibit behavior intermediate between Nd and Er
- Gd has a slightly smaller ionic radius than Nd but larger than Er, which correlates with Tc suppression rates

My prediction is that the Gd family will show a Tc suppression rate intermediate between the Nd and Er families - closer to the Er family but somewhat faster than Er and slower than Nd.

## Predicted Tc Values for Gd Family

Using interpolation between the Er and Nd curves, with Gd showing suppression rate closer to Er but slightly faster:
- Gd is expected to have slightly lower initial Tc than Er but higher than Nd
- The suppression rate will be between that of Er and Nd
