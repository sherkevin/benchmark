## Pattern Analysis

From the measurements, I can observe:

1. Both RE families show a decrease in Tc as Pr content increases
2. The Er family maintains higher Tc values at higher Pr concentrations compared to the Nd family
3. For the Er family: Tc decreases gradually with increasing Pr content
4. For the Nd family: Tc decreases more rapidly with increasing Pr content
5. Both families reach Tc = 0.0 K at high Pr content (0.500 and above)

## Reasoning for Gd Family Predictions

Based on the pattern observed, I need to determine where the Gd family falls in terms of Tc suppression by Pr doping. Looking at the lanthanide series:

- Er (atomic number 68) is towards the heavier end
- Nd (atomic number 60) is towards the lighter end  
- Gd (atomic number 64) is in between Er and Nd

Given this position in the lanthanide series, I expect the Gd family to have Tc suppression characteristics somewhere between the Er and Nd families. Since Gd is closer to Nd than to Er, I expect its Tc suppression to be somewhat similar to or slightly less severe than the Nd family.

Looking at the curves:
- At low Pr doping (0.030), Er: 93.9K, Nd: 85.1K → Gd likely around 88-90K
- At moderate Pr doping (0.150), Er: 82.9K, Nd: 40.7K → Gd likely around 60-65K
- At higher Pr doping, Gd should follow a similar suppression pattern

I'll interpolate based on the relative positions of Er and Nd suppression curves to estimate the Gd family values.
