### Pattern analysis:
1. All O6.xxxCa1Cu2Ba2Hg1 (Hg without Tl): high Tc
2. All O4.xxxCu1Ba2Hg1 (Hg without Tl): high Tc
3. O6Cu1Ba2Hg1Tl1 (Hg with Tl): very low Tc (0 K)
4. O10Ca2Cu3Ba2Tl2 (Tl without Hg, with Ca): high Tc
5. O6Cu1Cd0.250Ba2Tl1.750 (Tl without Hg, with Cd): low Tc

### Induced rule:
Materials are high Tc unless they contain both Hg and Tl together, or contain Tl with Cd instead of Ca.
