- Maximum Tc (T*) = 127.0 K for `O6.210Ca1Cu2Ba2Hg1` and `O6.220Ca1Cu2Ba2Hg1`.

## Step B — Filter Family B
- Threshold1 = T* - 32 = 95.0 K.
- Qualifying set Q1:

  - `O4.150Cu1Ba2Hg1` (97.0 K)

## Step C — Extrapolate Rule
- **Rule**: Compositions containing Hg and Cu, with oxygen content >= 6.0 and at least 4 elements, are predicted as high Tc (>= 90 K). Otherwise, low Tc.
