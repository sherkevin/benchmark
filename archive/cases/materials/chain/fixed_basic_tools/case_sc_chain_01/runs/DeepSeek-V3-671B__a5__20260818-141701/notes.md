## Step C: Induce Rule for Unmeasurable Materials
- **Observations from measurable data**:
  - High Tc materials (>90 K) typically have:
    - High oxygen content (e.g., O6.x).
    - Presence of `Hg` and `Ba`.
    - Complex compositions (5 elements).
  - Low Tc materials (<90 K) often have:
    - Lower oxygen content (e.g., O4.x).
    - Simpler compositions (4 elements).
- **Proposed rule**:
  - A material is **high** if:
    1. It contains `Hg`.
    2. It has at least 5 elements.
    3. The oxygen content is >= 6.0.
  - Otherwise, it is **low**.
