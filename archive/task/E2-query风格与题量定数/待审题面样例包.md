## 给专家的说明（含工作量口径）

我们在构建药物与材料发现领域的 agent 评测基准：agent 用检索与性质预测工具完成候选分子发现任务，答案源自公开数据库并已匿名化。请您审三件事：①题面术语与表述是否符合领域专家实际用法；②约束条件是否科学合理；③隐去答案与数值后题意是否仍清晰可执行。**不请您判**判分器实现与工具细节。**工作量说明（实际负担，请先知悉）**：全部 150 题都会过专家，但按级组织——**一级为全量扫读**（术语与约束是否合理，每题约 2–3 分钟）；**二级为 ≥30% 题的深审**（背书三问，按题目类型 × 领域分层抽取）；多专家并行，每人不超过 40 题——**不是要求您对每题都做深审**。另请就文末「术语用词征询表」给出标准术语意见。

## 样例 1 · 类型1 约束满足（药物 · CNS）

**题面**（按类型1 落盘句式 A + T + D 渲染）：

> Identify a central nervous system (CNS) candidate that satisfies all required properties (see the accompanying domain knowledge for exact criteria). You may use the retrieval tools to search literature and the candidate database, and the property-query interface to verify properties. Submit three fields: candidates (all candidates you examined), ranking (the single qualifying candidate as the sole element), and answer (your final recommended candidate ID).

**随题附的 domain_knowledge（约束外置字段，专家执笔区）**：

> - Aqueous solubility: the candidate's intrinsic solubility must fall within the absorption-compatible window specified in the property sheet (expressed as logS).
> - Structural scale: the candidate's heavy-atom size must stay within the complexity window for CNS drug-likeness specified in the property sheet (expressed as atom_count).

（占位稿、仅供术语征询：两条约束的数值窗为骨架占位，最终取值由药化专家执笔定稿，切分口径按 `(atom_count//5, round(logS))`；唯一解由多约束交集自然收敛，落盘时附穷举证明 |G|=1——指"满足约束的候选中目标量最优者唯一"，非"候选池仅 1 个成员"。）

| 附注项 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| 所属类型 | **fixed 类 · 类型1 约束满足**（唯一解，评过程 DAG） |
| 判分器要读的字段 | 答卷 `candidates`、`ranking`、`answer`（answer_schema.json 新字段，ADR-0014） |
| 该题考什么 | 逐项查证、按约束精确筛选、不跳步（过程检查点逐步核验） |
| 背书出处 | 待补（M10/ADR-0016：背书出处 100% 为硬门，须含真实论文标题 + DOI/URL；纯数据来源标注已被 `query_lint.py` 判为不合格） |

## 样例 3 · 类型2 约束最优（材料 · 超导，cuprate 族）

**题面**（按类型2 落盘句式 A + M + T + D 渲染）：

> Among cuprate superconductor candidates in the candidate database that satisfy all hard constraints (see the accompanying domain knowledge for exact criteria), find the best one. Scoring: candidates are ranked by superconducting transition temperature Tc (higher is better), and any candidate violating a hard constraint scores zero regardless of the objective. You may use the retrieval tools to search literature and the candidate database, and the property-query interface to verify properties. Submit three fields: candidates (all candidates you examined), ranking (qualifying candidates ordered best-first), and answer (your top recommendation).

**随题附的 domain_knowledge（约束外置字段，专家执笔区）**：

> - Family membership: the candidate must belong to the cuprate superconductor family as classified in the candidate database.
> - Superconducting validity: entries recorded with transition temperature 0 are non-superconducting samples and are excluded from the qualifying pool.
> - Objective: among the qualifying candidates in the candidate database, maximize the superconducting transition temperature Tc.

（占位稿、仅供术语征询：术语与约束表述待材料学专家核。**口径说明**：题面以 `in the candidate database` 限定范围、不对测量压力条件作任何限定（condition 字段实测仅 16 条有人工标注，若按压力条件过滤会令可用池只剩 15 个）；族分层用 E1 已建的 family 字段（9 族：Cuprate 874 / Ferrite 517 / Heavy_fermion 409 / Oxide 320 …），区分度与数据充足性均优于压力条件限定；排除 tc==0 是硬性前置（3DSC 含非超导样本，占 30.6%）。）

| 附注项 | 内容 |

| 判分器要读的字段 | 答卷 `candidates`、`ranking`、`answer`（ranking 从优到劣，answer 为首位推荐） |
| 该题考什么 | 硬门槛筛除 + 按目标量排序择优（排序正确性单独计分；cp5 是唯一考察多候选比较能力的检查点） |
