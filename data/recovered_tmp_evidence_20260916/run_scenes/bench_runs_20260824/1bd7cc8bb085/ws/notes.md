## Analysis of m05 and Congeneric Series
Looking at m05: COc1ccc(cc1)C(=O)NCCCCCCCCCCC (methoxyphenyl group with amide and long alkyl chain)

Molecules in the same congeneric series as m05 would have the same scaffold (methoxyphenyl group with amide) but differ only in the length of the N-alkyl chain.

From the data, I can see several molecules with the same core structure:
- m05: COc1ccc(cc1)C(=O)NCCCCCCCCCCC (11 carbon N-alkyl chain)
- m15: COc1ccc(cc1)C(=O)NCC (2 carbon N-alkyl chain)
- m17: COc1ccc(cc1)C(=O)NCCCCC (5 carbon N-alkyl chain)
- m22: COc1ccc(cc1)C(=O)NCCCCCC (6 carbon N-alkyl chain)
- m24: COc1ccc(cc1)C(=O)NCCC (3 carbon N-alkyl chain)
- m28: COc1ccc(cc1)C(=O)NCCCC (4 carbon N-alkyl chain)

Other scaffolds include:
- Nitrophenyl derivatives (NO2-c6h4-C(=O)N-R)
- Biphenyl derivatives (c6h4-c6h4-C(=O)N-R)
- Sugar-like derivatives (OCC(O)C(O)C(O)C(=O)N-R)

## Plan
1. Measure QED of several molecules from the same congeneric series as m05 to establish a trend
