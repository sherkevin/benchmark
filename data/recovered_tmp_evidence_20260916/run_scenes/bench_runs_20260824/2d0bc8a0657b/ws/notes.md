## Task 1: Predicting QED for m05

1. **Objective**: Predict the QED of `m05` (SMILES: `COc1ccc(cc1)C(=O)NCCCCCCCCCCC`).
2. **Approach**:
   - Identify molecules sharing the same scaffold as `m05` (congeneric series).
   - Measure QED for these molecules.
   - Fit a trend to the measured QED values and predict `m05`'s QED.

## Task 2: Selecting a Lead Molecule

1. **Objective**: Find a molecule with:
   - QED ≥ 0.6
   - `psa` ≥ 40
   - Not in the same congeneric series as `m05`.
2. **Approach**:
   - List the pool to identify candidates.
   - Measure QED for promising candidates.
   - Verify constraints are met.
