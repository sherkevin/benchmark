## Pattern Analysis

The suppression of Tc by Pr substitution follows a similar trend in both series, but the starting Tc values differ. The Er series starts at a higher Tc (93.9 K) compared to the Nd series (85.1 K).

Looking at the data, I can observe:
1. Both series show a decreasing Tc with increasing Pr content
2. The rate of decrease varies between the different rare earth elements
3. The Er series maintains superconductivity longer (higher Pr content needed to reach Tc = 0) compared to the Nd series

## Prediction Method for Gd Series

Based on the general trend observed in cuprates, Gadolinium (Gd) typically exhibits intermediate behavior between Er and Nd. Since Gd is positioned between Nd and Er in the lanthanide series, I expect its Tc vs Pr content behavior to fall between the two measured series.

From comparing Er (larger ionic radius) and Nd (smaller ionic radius), I predict that Gd (intermediate ionic radius) will have Tc values that fall between the Er and Nd series, closer to the average of the two.
