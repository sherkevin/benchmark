## Reflection on Selection Strategy
I selected 8 compounds based primarily on brain penetration potential (clogp 1-4, tpsa <90, hbd 0-1). However, finding compounds with logS in the narrow range of -3 to -2 proved challenging. 

Observations:
1. Many compounds with good brain penetration (bbb=1) had logS outside the target range:
   - Too soluble (logS > -2): z002746 (-0.63), z003001 (-0.14), z001999 (-1.52)
   - Too insoluble (logS < -3): z002003 (-3.6), z002011 (-4.13), z002015 (-3.82)

2. Only z002170 hit the sweet spot with logS = -2.92 (just within range).

3. Perhaps I could have tested a more diverse set of compounds with different clogp values to find more hits in the target solubility range.

4. The target profile (logS -3 to -2 AND bbb=1) appears to be quite specific, making it challenging to find multiple qualifying compounds.
