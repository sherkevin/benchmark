## Reasoning
1. **Tc Suppression with Pr Content (`x`)**: For both Er and Nd series, Tc decreases as the Pr content (`x`) increases. The suppression is more pronounced in the Nd series compared to the Er series.
2. **Critical Pr Content (`x_c`)**: The critical Pr content (`x_c`) at which Tc drops to 0 K is lower for the Nd series (~0.3) compared to the Er series (~0.5).
3. **Prediction for Gd Series**: The Gd series is expected to follow a similar trend, but the exact suppression rate may differ. Based on the observed trends:
   - The Gd series will likely have a Tc suppression rate intermediate between the Er and Nd series.
   - The critical Pr content (`x_c`) for the Gd series is estimated to be around ~0.4.
