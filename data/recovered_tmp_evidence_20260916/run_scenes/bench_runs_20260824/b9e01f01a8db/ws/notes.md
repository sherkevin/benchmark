Medicinal chemistry task notes

Task Overview:
- Need to find the QED for molecule m05 (which cannot be measured directly as it's not synthesized)
- Need to pick a molecule that meets three criteria:
  1. measured QED ≥ 0.6
  2. psa ≥ 40
  3. not in the same congeneric series as m05

Initial approach:
1. List the compound pool to understand the available molecules
2. Group by scaffold to identify which molecules are in the same series as m05
3. Determine the QED of m05 using trend analysis from its congeneric series
4. Find a suitable lead molecule meeting all criteria
