## Trend Analysis
- Tc decreases as Pr content (`x`) increases.
- The rate of Tc suppression varies with the rare earth (`Er` vs. `Nd`).
- For `Er` series, Tc drops to 0 K at `x = 0.8`.
- For `Nd` series, Tc drops to 0 K at `x = 0.5`.

## Prediction for Gd-series
- The Gd-series is expected to follow a similar trend.
- Given the intermediate behavior between `Er` and `Nd`, we predict:
  - Tc will drop to 0 K around `x = 0.6` for the Gd-series.
  - For `x < 0.6`, Tc will decrease linearly with `x`.
