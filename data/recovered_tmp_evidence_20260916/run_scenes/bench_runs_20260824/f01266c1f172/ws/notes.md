Materials Science Analysis: Predicting Tc for Gd Series Cuprates

Objective: Predict Tc values for Gd(1-x)Pr(x)Ba2Cu3O7 compositions that have not been synthesized.

Methodology:
1. Measured Tc values for Er and Nd series with varying Pr concentrations
2. Analyzed the suppression pattern of Tc with Pr doping
3. Used this pattern to predict Gd series behavior

Data Collected:
- Er series: Pr0.030Er0.970 (Tc=93.9K), Pr0.150Er0.850 (Tc=82.9K), Pr0.200Er0.800 (Tc=76.4K), Pr0.300Er0.700 (Tc=61.1K), Pr0.500Er0.500 (Tc=28.6K), Pr0.800Er0.200 (Tc=0.0K)
- Nd series: Pr0.030Nd0.970 (Tc=85.1K), Pr0.150Nd0.850 (Tc=40.7K), Pr0.200Nd0.800 (Tc=31.4K), Pr0.300Nd0.700 (Tc=5.6K), Pr0.500Nd0.500 (Tc=0.0K), Pr0.800Nd0.200 (Tc=0.0K)

Analysis:
The Pr suppression effect is stronger in the Nd series compared to the Er series. In other words, for the same Pr concentration, Tc is lower in Nd compounds than in Er compounds. This suggests that the nature of the rare earth element affects how strongly Pr suppresses superconductivity.

For the Gd series prediction, I considered that Gd is another rare earth element that, based on its position in the lanthanide series and magnetic properties, likely exhibits Pr suppression behavior intermediate between Er and Nd, but possibly slightly closer to Nd due to magnetic interactions (Gd3+ has a large magnetic moment like Nd3+).

Predictions:
Based on interpolation between Er and Nd behaviors, with a slight bias toward Nd behavior due to magnetic considerations, I estimated the Tc values for the Gd series compositions.
