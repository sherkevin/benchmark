The trend shows that QED initially increases from 2 to 3 carbons, then generally decreases as the chain length continues to increase. The 3-carbon chain has the highest QED value (0.7919), suggesting an optimal balance at this chain length.

For longer chains, the QED continues to decrease. From 5 carbon (0.7498) to 6 carbon (0.7377), there's a decrease of approximately 0.0121. 

Extrapolating this trend to the 11-carbon chain of m05: Since m05 has significantly longer chain (11 vs 6 carbons), we're extrapolating quite far beyond the measured range. Based on the decreasing trend, especially from 5 to 6 carbons, the QED for m05 should be lower than 0.7377.

Looking at the general trend of decreasing QED with increasing chain length beyond 3 carbons, and considering the rate of decrease, I estimate the QED of m05 (11 carbon chain) to be approximately 0.65.

## Prediction Method
