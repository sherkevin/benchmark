## Pattern Analysis

From the data, I observe that:

## Prediction Method for Gd Series

Based on literature and general trends in cuprates, Gd is expected to behave similarly to other rare earth elements. Since Gd has a slightly smaller ionic radius than Er but larger than Nd, I expect the Gd series to show intermediate behavior between the Er and Nd series. 

Looking at the parent compounds (REBa2Cu3O7):
- ErBa2Cu3O7 typically has higher Tc than NdBa2Cu3O7
- GdBa2Cu3O7 is expected to have Tc between these two values, likely closer to Er since Gd has similar ionic radius

For predicting the Gd series, I'll estimate based on the trend that Gd should show slightly faster Tc suppression than Er but slower than Nd. The initial Tc for Gd is estimated to be around 90K (between Er's ~93.9K and Nd's ~85.1K).
