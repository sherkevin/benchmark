D.6.2 Sensitivity Analysis
Table 7 presents the effect of the exploration param-
eter α on average reward, evaluated on the tuning
dataset described in Section 3.1. Consistent with
findings by (Li et al., 2010), the results exhibit an in-
verted U-shape. Small values of α result in insuffi-
cient exploration, limiting the algorithm’s ability to
discover optimal LLM-query matches. In contrast,
excessively high α values lead to over-exploration,
missing opportunities to maximize reward.
