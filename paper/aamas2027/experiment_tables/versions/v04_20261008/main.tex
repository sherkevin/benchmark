\reviewheading{Experimental results: core evidence}
{\small After the Decision: Learning to Adapt from Delayed Outcomes\hfill Review v04}

{\small\textbf{Common setup.} Four checkpoint-bound BBB providers, 157 held-out molecules, one call per input, and $D\in\{0,1,4\}$. Each method has 30 schedules and 4,710 recorded calls. Controlled drift flips the selected fit-best provider's label after tick 78. All paired intervals resample scaffold clusters.}
