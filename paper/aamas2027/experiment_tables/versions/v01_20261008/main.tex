\slot{Reading order and evidential roles}
\textbf{Core:} E1 tests the outcome claim; E2 tests its mechanism; E5 tests its efficiency. \textbf{Supporting diagnostics:} E3/E4 inspect cold start and update design on development data. \textbf{Secondary extension:} E6 examines post-call refusal with its own utility; it is not a pre-call online-routing comparison.

\slot{Units and scale}
The primary online cohort contains 157 distinct molecules, not 4,710 independent tasks. The latter is the recorded call count per method across repeated schedules. Across all methods, natural/stress totals are 270/150 streams. Development and held-out results have separate denominators. There is one provider call per input; no unselected output enters an online decision.

\slot{Placement in the paper}
Tables~\ref{tab:main-results}, \ref{tab:credit-ablation}, \ref{tab:cost-validity}, and \ref{tab:postcall-verification} use the approximately 7-inch two-column span. Tables~\ref{tab:learned-prior} and \ref{tab:centering-diagnostic} use the approximately 3.34-inch single-column width and can sit side by side. The result blocks use 8.5-point type, horizontal rules, aligned numeric columns, and no scaling box. This review pack is three pages; it is not a replacement for the full paper or a test of its final page count.
