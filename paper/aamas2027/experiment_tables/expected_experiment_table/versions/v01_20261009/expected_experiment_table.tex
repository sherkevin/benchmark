\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}
\small
\textbf{How to read this file.} Each numbered table follows the table order and row semantics of the current paper PDF. The numbers are conservative target values for planning the real runs. They are anchored to the current measured table, the current task contract, and the public cross-domain magnitude references in \texttt{~/work/reasonable\_results}; they are not copied public results. A target interval is a planning interval, not an observed confidence interval. The real paper may replace every cell only with a candidate-specific raw receipt and independently recovered statistic.

\textbf{Consistency rule.} The target keeps the intended pattern: natural routing is near Frozen and may be slightly worse; controlled feedback is where the proposed relay should beat matched Online SGD, with a positive margin at every declared delay; the ablation is weaker under the same pressure; heavier baselines may be slower; and the evaluator-only controls do not enter the deployable ranking.
