6. What would turn these goals into evidence?

   1. Close the natural-selection and attribution gaps first. Isolate E3 prior and E4 update effects, then combine them under a
      matched budget to establish one coherent method.
   2. Freeze the candidate, comparators and statistical plan before new evaluation. The inspected 157 molecules are not
      untouched confirmation; transfer target rates, not old counts.
   3. Retain negative results and independent recovery. Endpoint, lifecycle and agent extensions need separate evidence. The
      full scientific gate remains HOLD.
