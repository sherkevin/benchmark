\sectionhead{6. What would turn these goals into evidence?}
\begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
\item Close the natural-selection and attribution gaps first. Isolate E3 prior and E4 update effects, then combine them under a matched budget to establish one coherent method.
\item Freeze the candidate, comparators and statistical plan before new evaluation. The inspected 157 molecules are not untouched confirmation; transfer target rates, not old counts.
\item Retain negative results and independent recovery. Endpoint, lifecycle and agent extensions need separate evidence. The full scientific gate remains \textbf{HOLD}.
\end{enumerate}
