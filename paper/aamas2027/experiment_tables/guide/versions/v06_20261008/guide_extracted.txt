E1–E2 / body Table 4 and Figure 3: one shared budget
Let c be a recorded Relay correctness bit, h indicate Relay wrong/Frozen correct, and r indicate Relay correct/Frozen wrong. A
conditional repair budget is
                                               c′ = c + αh − β r,   0 ≤ α, β ≤ 1.
Case: for a harmed decision (c, h, r) = (0, 1, 0), α = .6 gives fractional score .6; for a rescued decision (1, 0, 1), β = .02 gives .98. These are budget
weights, not binary predictions. Assumption: the same fractions apply uniformly within every observed error stratum, in both conditions. All other
records are unchanged. Altered future feedback would require a full causal policy run.

A joint success criterion
A scientifically useful improvement should reduce harmful switches while retaining drift recovery, beat its own Frozen and
matched mechanism controls, and satisfy the kernel budget at the same time. E6 remains a separately frozen gate unless its joint
routing outputs are evaluated. Six independently improved means do not establish one successful candidate.
