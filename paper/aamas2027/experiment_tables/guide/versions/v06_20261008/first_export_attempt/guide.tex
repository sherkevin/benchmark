{\LARGE\bfseries Targets with an Error Model}\par
{\large Joint distributions, paired errors, and conditional requirements}\par

\textbf{M} values reproduce observations; blue \textbf{B} values answer \emph{what error changes would be required};
\textbf{T} values express goals. B is evaluator-side accounting, not a deployable strategy or a predicted future distribution.

\sect{E1--E2 / body Table 4 and Figure 3: one shared budget}
Let $c$ be a recorded Relay correctness bit, $h$ indicate Relay wrong/Frozen correct,
and $r$ indicate Relay correct/Frozen wrong. A conditional repair budget is
$$c'=c+\alpha h-\beta r,\qquad 0\leq\alpha,\beta\leq1.$$
\note{Case: for a harmed decision $(c,h,r)=(0,1,0)$, $\alpha=.6$ gives fractional score .6;
for a rescued decision $(1,0,1)$, $\beta=.02$ gives .98. These are budget weights, not binary predictions.
Assumption: the same fractions apply uniformly within every observed error stratum, in both conditions.
All other records are unchanged. Altered future feedback would require a full causal policy run.}

\sect{E3 / body Table 5: cold-start errors, not just a mean}
The development OOF folds partition 722 distinct molecules. Across the native panel, only mixed molecules
can change correctness when the provider menu is fixed. For this cohort,
$$\text{accuracy}=\frac{471+147q}{722},$$
\note{Case: $q$ is mixed-molecule selection accuracy. Corrected Frozen has $q=108/147=73.47\%$,
giving 579/722=80.19\%. Fresh has a different equation $(110+40q)/157$; Relay's $q=77.50\%$
gives 89.81\%. The cohort mix explains much of the level difference, not superior adaptation alone.}

\sect{A joint success criterion}
A scientifically useful improvement should reduce harmful switches while retaining drift recovery,
beat its \emph{own} Frozen and matched mechanism controls, and satisfy the kernel budget at the same time.
E6 remains a separately frozen gate unless its joint routing outputs are evaluated. Six independently improved means do not establish one successful candidate.

\sect{What has high-confidence support, and what still needs evidence}
\textbf{Verified empirical structure:} recorded denominators, provider-error overlap, paired rescue/harm,
fit/delay/phase strata, source hashes and table identities. These are finite-cohort facts, not generalization estimates.

\textbf{Exact conditional requirements:} given uniform $\alpha/\beta$ response, E1 pooled,
scaffold and stratum budgets are calculated from the same recorded molecules. E6 utility follows its
within-fit swap contract. Conditional validity does not establish attainable selectivity.

\textbf{Unresolved prediction:} no calibrated future outcome distribution, seed SD or predictive CI exists
for an unspecified new candidate. Null and adverse effects remain plausible; the previous learned
priors actually underperformed. The table should guide which mechanism to repair, not imply certainty of winning.

\sect{Before filling a prospective result matrix}
Freeze one candidate: features, prior, parameters, update rule, selected-only feedback, release clock,
failure semantics and gate. Then match its own Frozen, online baselines and attribution controls.
New arrival-order uncertainty requires full adaptive trajectories; unseen-scaffold generalization requires
untouched data. The 157 inspected molecules cannot become a new unseen confirmation cohort.
Write a calibrated predictive interval only after its model and coverage have been justified.

\sect{Evidence and reproducibility}
\note{Measured reference: \texttt{distribution\_audit\_v1/} contains 16 CSVs, 19 hashed sources,
and an independent standard-library extractor. Actual E1 records include 65,940 method/configuration/molecule rows;
E2 includes 28,260. Their Local and Relay terminal vectors match completely.
All seed dependencies are retained. \texttt{conditional\_requirements.json} stores the response assumptions,
fractional budget sums, fit/delay profiles, scaffold contrasts, and per-fit swap limits.
\texttt{build\_distribution\_guide.py} performs no training, policy replay, model inference or random sampling.}
