## Statistical unit and estimand

For each independently drawn uniform permutation, the study recomputes all adaptive selections and selected-only delayed feedback. Methods share the same permutation. The per-order result first averages **5 fixed priors × 2 fixed policy seeds × 3 delays = 30 configurations**. Its pooled denominator is 4,710; **the inferential sample size is 128 orders**, not 3,840 configurations, 61,440 streams, or 9,646,080 decisions.

Repeated molecules, scaffold dependence, repeated seed copies and fitted-prior dependence are retained inside each order. Conditional on these frozen inputs, they do not invalidate independence of the newly sampled order draws. They also do not create new information about provider training, checkpoint uncertainty, new scaffolds, or full policy randomness. Policy seeds 0/1 are reset and fixed across orders.

The primary estimand is the **order-law mean controlled MIRA-Relay minus Online SGD pooled correctness**. It is identifiable for this declared finite-cohort procedure because all relevant provider outcomes are cached and each adaptive history was actually recomputed by the recorded study. It does not identify performance under a natural deployment chronology.

Controlled stress flips the fit-best provider at new ticks 78–156. Consequently, changing the order also changes **which molecules receive altered provider labels**. Controlled variation concerns this combined order and tick-triggered stress law. It is not pure ordering variation while holding damaged molecule outcomes fixed. The final source states this correctly.

## Joint dependence is consequential

Independence can overstate or understate the spread of a contrast. The page's comparison correctly teaches this without proposing the independent column as an alternative valid interval. Natural Frozen is constant across orders, so its correlation is undefined; a numerical zero covariance does not justify inventing a correlation.

The covariance is observed co-variation of methods under a common order/stress law. It does not itself identify a biological error mechanism, explain why one updater succeeds, or demonstrate novelty. Preserving provider vectors and common histories prevents artificial independence; it does not turn descriptive covariance into causal attribution.

## Prioritized remaining objections and decision tests

These limit broader claims; they do not block the scoped reference PASS.

1. **Future candidate remains untested.** The measured distribution is for existing policies. An improved prior/update/gate cannot inherit its SD, covariance, wins or mean interval. The original `alpha/beta` budgets remain exact conditional accounting under their stated assumption, not transformed adaptive trajectories. Decision test: freeze one common candidate and evaluate its own Frozen, matched update controls and own permutation on common histories; report paired harm/rescue signatures, not six independently favorable totals.

2. **The natural result resists a blanket success claim.** Relay's pooled natural mean is below Frozen, LinUCB, BayesianRouter and the credit permutation. The independently recovered natural Relay−permutation pooled mean is **−0.341030 pp**. Its equal-scaffold Relay−Frozen mean is **+0.211029 pp**, a different weighting target; the equal-scaffold Local−permutation mean is **+0.051768 pp**, with exploratory pointwise mean interval **[−0.087758, +0.191295] pp**. Decision test: retain the prespecified pooled and scaffold targets together; never select the favorable weighting after inspection.

3. **Stress robustness is specific to the imposed failure law.** A fit-best-provider label flip at tick 78 is not evidence that this provider actually degrades that way in pharmaceutical deployment. Variation mixes adaptive response with allocation of corrupted outcomes. If a later claim requires the causal effect of order alone, a separate prespecified comparison must hold the affected molecule/provider set fixed across orders. That experiment is not necessary to estimate the current declared law.

4. **Controls establish a limited mechanism contrast.** Correct versus fixed-permutation credit changes the algorithm's assignment of feedback under a severe stress. This supports that matched attribution intervention in the recorded procedure. It does not uniquely attribute all advantages over SGD to context use, rule out parameter or representation choices, or establish a general feedback architecture claim. Future mechanism tests must use the candidate's own controls and preserve legal receipt release; timing changes need full trajectories, since their downstream effects cannot be bounded by provider-level receipt coverage counts.

5. **Direction is weaker than useful improvement.** The primary criterion tests positive mean and majority-order wins at zero. Secondary outcomes are exploratory and pointwise, without simultaneous coverage or post-selection guarantees. For a claim of practically valuable improvement, freeze a justified nonzero margin and its utility/cost tradeoff before confirmation; evaluate that margin and adverse-order behavior. The existing result cannot retrospectively supply a preregistered practical threshold.

6. **Untouched confirmation remains necessary for new-scaffold claims.** The 157 molecules were viewed before this study. Sampling their order adds valid order-law evidence but does not make their identities untouched. A new candidate's training uncertainty, unseen-scaffold generalization and deployed cost require their own evidence. The broader scientific gate remains HOLD; this reference audit does not reopen it.

The recorded study is feasible with its cached inputs: no new provider inference or refits were needed, its completed run and independent verification fit the declared resource envelope, and compact order metrics are sufficient for this statistical reduction. Feasibility of a future candidate with changed representations, ranking or live tools is still unknown.
