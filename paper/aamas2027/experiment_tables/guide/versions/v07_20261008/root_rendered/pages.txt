                                                            s2A−B = s2A + s2B − 2 Cov(A, B).
Case: A and B are MIRA and SGD accuracy on each of the 128 matched orders. The actual paired SD is 2.40pp; discarding their covariance gives
3.22pp. With Frozen, negative covariance instead increases paired spread.
Three distinct statistical objects. The recorded approximate 95% Monte Carlo interval for the controlled MIRA–SGD mean is
[4.32,5.16]pp; the empirical 2.5/97.5% order quantiles are [0.45,8.61]pp. The recorded exact interval for its win probability is
[93.30,99.51]% (125/128 wins). None is a predictive interval for a new method. The original-order budgets on page 2 are not
transported here. Reproduction: distribution_audit_arrival_v1/ preserves order means, paired counts, covariance matrices and 654 hashed
sources. The full run’s independent_full_v2/ verification closes all 128 orders; the earlier failed verifier is retained. No new policy was executed
for this Guide.
