6. What would turn these goals into evidence?
   1. Prioritize natural selection gain and mechanism separation. The current natural null is the main missing link; higher
      controlled scores alone do not close it.
   2. Keep E3 capacity and E4 update interventions separate, then test their combination with matched budgets. Filling each
      table independently does not establish one coherent method.
   3. Freeze candidate, comparators, metric and statistical plan before new evaluation. The inspected 157 molecules cannot be
      relabeled as untouched confirmation. Target rates transfer as goals; the old counts do not.
   4. Preserve negatives, raw events and independent recovery. Broader endpoint, lifecycle and agent claims need their own
      evidence. The full scientific gate remains HOLD.
