## Natural-language structure

Use four aligned regions on one horizontal baseline. Region A is the pre-call information boundary: molecule, endpoint, current eligible menu, and released history enter the selector; unselected provider outputs are shown as a faint crossed-out ghost outside the boundary. Region B is the decision state: a grey immutable fit-only fallback and a blue MIRA-Relay state with family residual and provider bias produce one selected provider. Region C is the single call lifecycle: selected output and runtime status are recorded, then an amber frozen endpoint judge evaluates chemical correctness only after termination. Region D is the delayed release loop: the terminal event waits in a muted-purple queue until `tau_t = t + 1 + D`, then enters released history and the next selector state. Show the released challenger outcome entering the delayed queue; no correctness arrow may enter the current selector or current action. Fallback outcomes go only to the grey diagnostic ledger. The current action must have no incoming correctness arrow. The fit-only fallback is an immutable cold-start reference, not a safety guarantee.

## Prompt to copy

Design a clean, publication-ready flat vector framework figure for a top multi-agent systems paper, 1600×540 canvas, white background, one horizontal reading direction, four bounded regions, strict alignment and large readable labels. Region A: “Before the call” with a molecule, endpoint, eligible provider menu, and “released history”; place unselected provider outputs outside the boundary as a faint crossed-out ghost labelled “not observed”. Region B: “MIRA-Relay selector” with a grey immutable “fit-only fallback” and a navy-blue “family residual + provider bias” state, both feeding exactly one selected provider. Region C: “After termination” with one selected tool output and runtime status, then an amber “frozen endpoint judge” that emits chemical correctness only after termination; keep “system failure” and “chemical correctness” as separate labels. Region D: “Delayed release” with a muted-purple queue labelled “tau_t = t + 1 + D”, then “released state” and an arrow to “next decision”. Show the released challenger outcome entering the delayed queue; no correctness arrow may enter the current selector or current action. Fallback outcomes go only to the grey diagnostic “ledger only” branch. Use only navy #2E5DA1 for MIRA-Relay, light grey #B8C0CC for frozen/baseline, amber #D99000 for the terminal record and frozen judge, muted purple #6B4C9A for pending and delayed release, and black text. Solid arrows are observed events; dashed arrows are waiting/control flow; ghosted elements are unavailable observations. No extra call, no refusal branch, no immediate feedback, no fake result numbers, no gradients, no 3-D, no shadows, no decorative clip art, no dense equations, no tiny text. At the final paper width, use at least 8 pt equivalent; shorten secondary labels instead of shrinking them.

## Negative prompt

Avoid a generic neural-network block diagram, circular arrows that imply instantaneous learning, a second provider call, a full output panel, arrows from unselected outputs, red/green correctness encoding, crowded prose, or illegible generated typography.
