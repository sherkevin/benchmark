# Current-paper uncertainty scope audit

Date: 2026-10-08. Scope: read existing paper, receipts, summaries, and statistical code; no experiment, bootstrap, policy replay, or provider call executed. Only this review file was written. Reviewed `main.tex` SHA-256: `831149e1f05b17a6943dafa8d309ddb98c4643eef5083a1a82e820fc54f47a72`.

## Verdict

**The conditional-second-call v1/v2 order-law defect does not occur in the current main, source-credit, or post-call interval calculations.** All three resample **already recorded outcomes**. None passes the sampled scaffold list to a policy, release queue, or budget controller. The concatenation order therefore cannot change actions or introduce an alternative arrival process in these calculations.

There is a separate **P1 inference-scope issue for the adaptive main/source-credit results**: outcomes in different scaffold clusters share a feedback-adaptive history. The existing bootstrap preserves within-scaffold repetitions but does not establish independence between scaffolds, account for the effect of a different cohort on later decisions, or justify nominal 95% coverage for adaptive-policy performance on new streams. The numerical intervals remain reproducible descriptions of scaffold reweighting of the recorded trajectories. This is a limit on their inferential interpretation, not evidence that the recorded gains or paired comparisons are numerically wrong.

The **static post-call gate is unaffected by that adaptive-history issue**. Its gate-versus-always-accept comparison is compatible with conventional conditional scaffold inference if the sampled scaffolds can be treated as independent, representative evaluation units. The comparison with the one fixed random acceptance draw has a narrower, explicitly conditional interpretation; it is not a randomization test or uncertainty over all same-coverage random draws. The current paper already states most of these post-call limits.

### Main versus source-credit weighting

Let $d_{si}$ be the **recorded** correctness difference for molecule $i$ in matched configuration $s$, and let $\bar d_i$ average that molecule's 30 configurations. Let $I_g$ contain the molecules in scaffold $g$. The two point statistics are:

$$
\widehat\Delta_{\mathrm{main}}=\frac{1}{157}\sum_{i=1}^{157}\bar d_i,
\qquad
\widehat\Delta_{\mathrm{credit}}=\frac{1}{124}\sum_{g=1}^{124}\frac{1}{|I_g|}\sum_{i\in I_g}\bar d_i.
$$

Task instantiation: Local−Frozen under controlled drift is +14.18 pp in the pooled main statistic and +14.40 pp in the equal-scaffold statistic. In natural outcomes the corresponding values are 0.00 and +0.10 pp. Those differences follow from weighting; they do not indicate contradictory receipts. The paper's table note correctly distinguishes them. Both bootstraps retain repeated configurations within scaffold and use the same weighting as their own point statistic.

## 2. What the repetitions and order actually mean

The five folds supply five fitted priors evaluated on the same 157 confirmation molecules; the two seed values and three delays produce 30 schedules per method. The fitting and loop structure is explicit in [the natural runner](/Users/jingwu/work/benchmark/src/probe_headless/run_provider_fresh_confirmation_v1.py:414). Random and BayesianRouter consume a policy RNG seed; Frozen and MIRA-Relay do not use it to create a fresh molecule order ([policy construction](/Users/jingwu/work/benchmark/src/probe_headless/run_provider_fresh_confirmation_v1.py:263), [Relay construction](/Users/jingwu/work/benchmark/src/probe_headless/run_provider_fresh_confirmation_v1.py:426)). The source-credit runner explicitly labels the two copies **deterministic schedule replicas, not seed robustness** ([configuration](/Users/jingwu/work/benchmark/src/probe_headless/run_fresh_mechanism_ablation_v2.py:319)).

All these runs enumerate the fixed manifest order. They release due feedback and then choose the current provider ([main stream loop](/Users/jingwu/work/benchmark/src/probe_headless/run_provider_fresh_confirmation_v1.py:318), [credit stream loop](/Users/jingwu/work/benchmark/src/probe_headless/run_fresh_mechanism_ablation_v2.py:165)). Controlled drift attaches to the original tick, from 78 onward; the statistical scripts retain the resulting recorded labels rather than relocating the drift boundary on a new sequence.

Therefore the current bootstrap does **not** treat 4,710 calls or 30 schedules as independent confirmation samples. That is a correct protection against repetition-based pseudoreplication. It also does not estimate training-set uncertainty, an independent 30-run variance, provider/checkpoint uncertainty, or arrival-order robustness. “Two seeds” in the main protocol should not be read as two random arrival streams.

## 3. Remaining dependence and counterfactual limits

### Adaptive main and source-credit results — P1

A released outcome from one scaffold can change the selected provider for a later molecule in a different scaffold. Resampling the first scaffold while holding the later outcome fixed does not reproduce how the adaptive policy would behave on that altered cohort. Scaffold-disjoint chemistry does not remove this algorithm-induced dependence. Keeping a scaffold's own repetitions together handles a different source of dependence.

**Constructed task case, not an observed result:** scaffold A supplies a released failure that lowers provider P's score. A later molecule in scaffold B is consequently routed to Q. A bootstrap sample that omits A still carries B's recorded Q outcome. That is coherent reweighting of the existing record, but not a simulated run on a cohort where A was absent. No ordering of those fixed values repairs that difference.

Allowed interpretation without additional assumptions: paired performance on the sealed trajectories, plus central 95% empirical scaffold-resampling ranges showing sensitivity to which recorded scaffold outcomes receive weight. For inference on new adaptive runs, one must declare the random unit and target population and justify the dependence treatment—for example through an applicable stability/dependence argument or genuinely independent trajectory units. Neither is supplied by the cited bootstrap code. This review does not prove that nominal coverage fails; it finds that the current calculation does not establish it.

This matters because the table headers and main prose say “95% CI,” and the abstract cites the positive interval ([abstract](/Users/jingwu/work/benchmark/paper/aamas2027/main.tex:209)). Limiting the substantive claim to the declared drift replay is appropriate but does not by itself supply a sampling model. A frozen cohort/order with fixed policy seeds has an exactly observed finite-cohort effect; a nonzero bootstrap range requires an explicitly different target, such as scaffold-composition sensitivity or a justified superpopulation effect.

### Static post-call result — no adaptive-order defect; P2 scope check

The fit-only provider and threshold remain fixed. A molecule's confidence determines acceptance independently of earlier confirmation outcomes ([gate construction](/Users/jingwu/work/benchmark/src/probe_headless/run_fresh_postcall_verifier_v1.py:116)). Consequently, reweighting its recorded gate/always-accept outcomes is equivalent to evaluating those same fixed decisions on the reweighted records. A conventional cluster bootstrap is plausible for **fixed fitted gates on independently sampled scaffold clusters**; representative scaffold sampling is still an assumption, not proved by disjointness. The interval does not include retraining or threshold-selection uncertainty. The reused, previously inspected cohort makes this secondary evidence, as [the paper already states](/Users/jingwu/work/benchmark/paper/aamas2027/main.tex:496).

The random comparator selects exactly the confidence gate's accepted count **once per original full cohort/fold**, without replacement ([selection code](/Users/jingwu/work/benchmark/src/probe_headless/run_fresh_postcall_verifier_v1.py:125)). Its memberships then remain fixed in every bootstrap sample. Exact coverage matching need not survive scaffold reweighting. **Constructed case:** if the gate accepts only a molecule from scaffold A and the random draw accepts only one from scaffold B, both originally accept one; sampling A twice gives gate count two and random count zero. The actual audit computes the two accepted-accuracy denominators separately, as it should for its recorded-outcome statistic. It does not redraw a matched-coverage null for each new sample.

Thus the reported gate−random interval describes sensitivity of the **realized paired comparison**, conditional on the recorded random memberships. It cannot support a claim of randomization significance or seed robustness against the expected same-coverage null. The paper's explicit “fixed random draws” language already prevents the strongest overreading; preserving it is essential. Gate−always-accept utility has no random-membership complication and is the cleaner post-call uncertainty comparison.

## 4. Priority and reusable outcome

1. **GO: preserve every existing point estimate, interval value, and raw artifact.** No evidence here calls for applying the conditional-second-call “new order law” diagnosis to the current tables or for rerunning them under the old block replay.
2. **P1 / scientific gate still open:** identify the intended inferential target of the adaptive main and source-credit intervals. Until coverage is justified for that target, use them as recorded-trajectory scaffold-reweighting evidence; do not let a positive interval alone establish population significance, equivalence, or new-stream reliability. A descriptive rename alone does not close the broader requirement for meaningful uncertainty supporting a generalizable claim.
3. **P2:** keep post-call conditioning and secondary status explicit; distinguish fixed-draw cohort sensitivity from a repeated-randomization null. No new order experiment is required to resolve this narrow audit.

Reusable material is already local: the matched terminal-difference extraction, preserved within-scaffold repeated schedules, explicit main/credit weighting distinction, and post-call fixed-gate paired utility calculation are sound building blocks for a target-specific uncertainty analysis. The missing item is the inferential contract for the adaptive claim, not receipt reconstruction. No broader method decision or paper edit was made in this audit.
